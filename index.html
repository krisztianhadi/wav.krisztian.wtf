Soundwaves

In this little corner of the internet I share some of the noise that some people might call music, made by me.

For some years now I've been messing around with different musical instruments from bass guitar to modular synthesizers. I'm far from being a professional musician, but I truly enjoy learning and working with different instruments, and most importantly having fun in the process.

Most of the time what I truly enjoy is a generative approach, we can call it a conversation between man and machine. A collaboration of human feelings and engineered circuits to create something new and unique.

For the time being I'm actually building out this page, you can find my noise in two places:

  • Ent Bien on Soundcloud
    My generative ambient project, mostly made with my portable modular synthesizer.
  • BackpackJams on Instagram
    A more random collection of improvised jams.

Enjoy.
